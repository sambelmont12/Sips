Frequently Asked Questions about Sips Coffee & Café in Rochester

Where exactly is Sips Coffee & Café located in Rochester?

You'll find us at 149 Pattonwood Drive, Rochester, NY 14617 — right where Irondequoit meets Charlotte near the mouth of the Genesee River.

Do you serve locally roasted coffee?

Yes! We partner with Rochester roasters for espresso, pour-overs, and seasonal blends. Ask about our current single-origin feature.

Is there parking available?

We offer free on-site parking and plenty of street parking on Pattonwood Drive, making it easy to grab coffee before heading to Seabreeze or Durand Beach.

Can I work or study at the café?

Absolutely. We have reliable Wi-Fi, plenty of outlets, and quiet morning hours — perfect for remote work, tutoring sessions, or studying for classes at MCC or RIT.
